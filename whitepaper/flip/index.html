FLIP is a utility token that cannot be bought from a curve, minted by the team, or farmed passively. Every circulating FLIP survived a coinflip before it was minted: the daily coinflip, or the single survival flip that settles a Degenerette FLIP payout. The token's entire supply trajectory is downstream of player engagement and randomness.

There is no pre-mine. The vault and sDGNRS each hold a seeded coinflip stake of 200,000 FLIP a day for the game's first 20 days, re-armed for 20 days at every century level, and each day's seed mints only if that day's flip wins. The vault's seed is the team's position: it only has value if the game succeeds and players engage. Beyond that, the circulating supply starts at zero and grows only when players participate in the game and survive coinflips. The contracts are immutable.

The Mint Gate: The Coinflip

Every day, FLIP holders can stake into one 50/50 coinflip shared by the whole game: a single bit of the day's Chainlink VRF word decides every stake at once. If you win, you get your principal back plus a bonus. If you lose, your principal is burned permanently and you receive 1 WWXRP as a consolation.

Win Bonus Distribution

The bonus on wins is rolled from VRF entropy, not fixed:

OutcomeProbabilityBonusTotal Payout
Unlucky5%50%1.5x principal
Normal90%78% to 115%1.78x to 2.15x principal
Lucky5%150%2.5x principal

Expected Value

The mean reward on a win is 96.85%. With a 50% win probability:

E[outcome] = 0.50 x (1 + 0.9685) + 0.50 x 0 = 0.984

Base coinflip EV: 98.4%. The coinflip is intentionally slightly negative-EV in FLIP terms. The ~1.6% expected loss is a structural burn. But participation is the point: coinflip winnings build BAF (Big-Ass Flip) score, and BAF jackpots pay out of the ETH futurepool.

On bonus days (level 1's purchase days, the second day of each jackpot phase, and the first purchase day after a one-day turbo phase), wins pay an extra 0.02x, or 0.06x on an x0 level.

Minimum Stake

100 FLIP per deposit. Deposits always target the next day's window. You cannot deposit and resolve in the same VRF cycle. This index-based separation makes it impossible to front-run the coinflip outcome.

Claim Windows

Winnings must be claimed within 365 days (180 days for the first ever claim). Unclaimed winnings expire. Players with auto-rebuy enabled have no expiry as long as auto-rebuy remains active.

FLIP Credits: The Gateway

A FLIP credit is not yet FLIP. It is an entry ticket to tomorrow's coinflip. When you earn FLIP credits, they are queued for the next daily coinflip window. If the coinflip wins, those credits become claimable FLIP at the win multiplier (1.5x to 2.5x). If it loses, they are burned.

Most FLIP earned from gameplay enters as credits, meaning it must survive a 50/50 coinflip before it exists as a transferable token. But because wins pay 1.5x to 2.5x (mean ~1.97x), the expected output of 1,000 FLIP in credits is ~984 liquid FLIP. The coinflip filters credits into liquid FLIP at roughly 1:1, with a small ~1.6% structural burn.

Sources of FLIP Credits

  • ETH ticket purchases: 100 FLIP in credits per ticket (10% of face value), plus 50 per ticket on a buy of 10 or more tickets.
  • FLIP ticket purchases: No FLIP credit. Buying with FLIP is a pure burn.
  • Recycle bonus: Spending at least 3 tickets' worth of claimable winnings on tickets or luckbox earns 10% of the recycled value in FLIP credits.
  • Craps battles: Every craps payout, including the daily jackpot battle's, is paid as FLIP credits.
  • Level 1 draw: Level 1's purchase days pay a trait-matched FLIP draw in place of the ETH drip.
  • Luckbox FLIP prizes: Flat FLIP prizes enter as credits. FLIP Degenerette spins from a luckbox pay after one survival flip.
  • Affiliate commissions: All affiliate payouts are FLIP credits, subject to a mandatory coinflip. Commissions on luckbox purchases by high-activity buyers taper to 25% of the base rate.
  • Mining bounty: Whoever runs the day's protocol work through mineFlip() earns FLIP credits for the gas burned above the first million per call, rising the longer the work has waited.
  • Quest rewards: Each of the two daily quests pays 100 FLIP in credits, up to 200 per day. Completing the level quest pays 800.

Every one of these sources is filtered through a coinflip before reaching circulation. A losing day burns every pending credit in the game at once; a winning day pays all of them the variable win bonus (mean ~1.97x). Expected pass-through rate: ~98.4% of the credited value survives as circulating, liquid FLIP.

Where FLIP Burns

FLIP has many sinks. Each one removes tokens from circulation permanently.

  • Coinflip losses: A losing day burns every stake in the game at once. In expectation the net burn is ~1.6% of total staked per cycle, accounting for the win bonus.
  • FLIP ticket purchases: Players can buy tickets with FLIP at 1,000 FLIP per ticket (250 per entry, 4 entries per ticket), from the moment a level's prize target is met through its jackpot days. The FLIP is burned completely.
  • Decimator burns: Minimum 2,000 FLIP. Burned to build a stack in a craps battle for a share of the futurepool at x5 levels (5, 15, ..., 85) and every x00. Degen score multiplies the stack from 1.0x to 2.0x.
  • Craps battles: Entry fees and day passes are burned up front.
  • Degenerette FLIP cards: 100 FLIP minimum per spin. The stake burns win or lose.
  • Growth side bets: 1,000 FLIP per bet, burned at placement.
  • ReverseFlip nudge: Costs 100 FLIP base, rising 50% per queued nudge (100, 150, 225, ...). Burns FLIP to shift the next daily VRF word by +1 before it is requested.
  • Curses: Lifting a cursed player's curse costs 100 FLIP. A deity holder's smite costs 200.

The Decimator

The decimator is a FLIP-burn craps battle for a share of the futurepool. Players burn FLIP to build a stack, the stack scales the score of their craps run, and the top-scoring entries split the pot in ETH.

When It Fires

The burn window opens when level x4 (or x99 before a century) meets its prize target and closes when the milestone level meets its own. There is no decimator at x95 and none at GAMEOVER. Prize pools:

The Stack

Minimum burn: 2,000 FLIP. Each wallet holds one entry; a later burn tops up the same stack, and the board from the last burn is the one it plays. Each burn adds the FLIP burned (plus any quest or boon bonus) × 0.9 for each day since the window opened × a degen score multiplier from 1.0x at zero activity to 2.0x at the effective cap. sDGNRS enters automatically on the window's opening day.

The Battle

At resolution a random half of the entries, at most 1,000, each play one craps run on the same dice with their own board. An entry's score is its stack times the peak bankroll its run reaches. The top 10% win, at least 20 and at most 200, never more than half the entries.

Prize Split

95% of the pool splits equally among the winners, and the champion takes the other 5% on top. Prizes pay in ETH, except that the champion takes half its prize in Whale Passes and, once an equal share reaches the 2.25 ETH cost of 0.5 Whale Pass, every other place below the champion is paid in Whale Passes.

The Price Ratchet

Ticket prices in ETH rise as the game progresses through levels. Ticket prices in FLIP stay fixed at 1,000 FLIP per ticket, redeemable from the moment a level's prize target is met through its jackpot days. This creates a natural price floor that rises with the ticket price through each century.

The utility floor needs no AMM or oracle. As long as the game progresses, 1,000 FLIP buys one ticket, so the ETH value of a FLIP tracks the ticket price: it rises 6x from x01 to x00 within each century, then resets with the price.

FLIP and the Jackpots

The Daily Jackpot Battle

One craps jackpot battle runs every day in both phases. Paid entrants and day-pass holders play alongside awarded seats, and the game adds a baseline of FLIP worth 0.5% of the previous level's recorded prize pool, with a floor of 150,000 FLIP through level 2's purchase phase and 50,000 after, scaled by the day's entry fee against the standard 8,000 FLIP. Winnings are paid as FLIP credits.

Far-Future Tickets

Tickets queued for future levels cannot win their own level's ETH jackpots until that level arrives. They still take part in the daily jackpot battle: its awarded seats go to ticket holders drawn from levels +1 to +99, one seat per 10,000 FLIP of that baseline, at most 500 a day.

This gives future tickets time-value. The earlier you acquire a ticket at a distant level, the more daily seat draws it enters before that level's ETH jackpots begin. A ticket at level 80 bought during level 10 is eligible for 70 levels' worth of draws that a ticket bought at level 75 would not see.

BAF Eligibility

The Big-Ass Flip jackpot fires every 10 levels and pays out of the futurepool in ETH and tickets, but only if that day's coinflip wins. On a loss the pool stays in the futurepool and every BAF score in the window becomes claimable as WWXRP. BAF score is cumulative coinflip winnings over the 10-level window. The top coinflip winner, a size-weighted draw over the final purchase day's coinflip deposits, and a random pick from the 3rd and 4th leaderboard slots share 20% of the pool; the other 80% is scattered across ticket holders in sampled rounds, half of them drawn from tickets queued 2 to 99 levels ahead, where a sampled holder needs a positive BAF score to win.

Coinflip Auto-Rebuy

Coinflip auto-rebuy is automatic coinflip compounding. When enabled, coinflip winnings are not withdrawn as liquid FLIP. Instead, the payout rolls forward as your stake for the next day's coinflip. Your balance compounds day over day, with an optional take-profit amount: every whole multiple of it in a win banks to your claimable FLIP, and the remainder rolls.

Coinflip auto-rebuy has no requirements: any player can toggle it on, with optional take-profit thresholds that skim winnings off the rolling stake (or zero for full compounding).

Carry Bonus

Each time winnings are recycled forward into the next coinflip, a flat 0.75% carry bonus is added, rounded down to whole FLIP.

The result is a compounding loop: coinflip winnings re-stake automatically, earning the carry bonus each cycle. The longer you stay in, the larger your rolling position grows, until a losing day burns it. FLIP never leaves the system unless you hit a take-profit threshold or turn off auto-rebuy. The afKing subscription, which auto-buys tickets or luckbox, is a separate feature and does not use the coinflip.

FLIP in the Vault

The Degenerus Vault issues two share classes. DGVE (Vault Eth) claims ETH and stETH reserves. DGVF (Vault Flip) claims FLIP reserves.

Virtual Deposit Mechanism

The vault's FLIP is virtual. FLIP minted or transferred to the vault leaves circulation and is recorded as a mint allowance on the FLIP contract. When DGVF holders burn shares to claim, FLIP is minted from this allowance on demand.

The vault starts with no FLIP reserve. Its seeded coinflip stake, 200,000 FLIP a day for the first 20 days and again for 20 days at every century level, adds to the allowance only through winning flips, so its value is entirely dependent on the game succeeding.

The Vault as Player

The vault itself operates as a player in the game. It holds a genesis deity pass (one ticket, 4 entries, per level, with a deity's degen score), runs an afKing subscription that buys one luckbox a day from its claimable winnings, and takes the affiliate commission on purchases by players with no referrer, split with sDGNRS by a coin toss. The FLIP the vault earns feeds its FLIP reserves, growing the value backing each DGVF share.

FLIP in the Affiliate System

All affiliate commissions in Degenerus are paid as FLIP credits, not liquid FLIP. This means affiliate income must survive a coinflip before becoming transferable tokens.

Why FLIP Instead of Direct Payment

This is an intentional variance filter. Professional affiliates who expect guaranteed income self-select out. Affiliates who enjoy gambling and match the protocol's target audience self-select in. Fewer affiliates, but the right ones.

Commission Rates

Affiliate rewards are calculated as a percentage of the ETH-equivalent transaction value, converted to FLIP:

SourceRate
Fresh ETH (through level 3)25% of transaction value
Fresh ETH (levels 4+)20% of transaction value
Recycled ETH (claimable)5% of transaction value
afKing subscription purchasesFlat 7% of spend, split 75/20/5 without a roll

The commission is resolved by a winner-take-all roll: on each referred purchase, one party takes the whole scaled reward. The direct affiliate wins with 75% probability, upline 1 with 20%, and upline 2 with 5%, converging to a 75/20/5 split over many purchases. The roll preserves expected value while reducing gas.

Kickback

An affiliate with a custom code can return 0-25% of each reward to the referred player as FLIP credits. Purchases by players with no referrer pay the commission to the vault or sDGNRS on a coin toss.

Supply Dynamics

There is no inflation rate and no farming. The only scheduled issuance is the vault and sDGNRS seed stakes, and they mint only on winning flips. FLIP supply is determined entirely by the balance between minting (coinflip wins) and many sinks. That balance shifts depending on the current game level and how players choose to use their FLIP.

The Equilibrium

The coinflip produces a ~1.6% net burn per cycle. On its own, this is a slow drain. But the sinks pulling FLIP out of circulation vary with player behavior:

  • Every FLIP ticket purchase (1,000 per ticket) is a permanent burn
  • Every decimator entry (2,000+ FLIP) is a permanent burn
  • Every craps battle entry and day pass is a permanent burn
  • Every ReverseFlip nudge (100+ FLIP, escalating) is a permanent burn
  • Luckbox taper cuts affiliate commission on luckbox purchases by high-activity buyers to a quarter of the base rate

Meanwhile, FLIP credits flow in from ETH ticket purchases, quest rewards, jackpot and craps battles, affiliate commissions, and mining bounties. The rate of credit generation depends on how much ETH is being spent, how many quests are completed, and how many jackpots fire. The coinflip converts credits to liquid FLIP at ~98.4% efficiency.

The result is a dynamic equilibrium. At lower levels where ticket prices are cheap and activity is high, credit generation can outpace burns, growing the supply. At higher levels where ETH prices rise (and FLIP ticket purchases become more attractive relative to ETH), the burn sinks intensify. The supply contracts or expands based on the collective behavior of all players at any given moment.

The supply curve depends entirely on player behavior. More sink usage means less circulating supply. Less supply with constant or growing utility demand means the price ratchet tightens. A tighter ratchet makes FLIP more valuable as a game input. But more valuable FLIP also means more incentive to earn it through credit sources. The equilibrium point shifts with every level and every player decision.

Trust Model

The FLIP contract is immutable. There are no admin keys, no proxy upgrades, no owner functions. The mint paths are hardcoded at deployment to the game and coinflip contracts, plus the vault drawing down its own allowance. While the game runs, no FLIP reaches circulation without surviving a coinflip. At GAMEOVER the contract adds 1018 FLIP to the vault's mint allowance, a one-time overhang that marks the token as finished. No address can change the burn mechanics or the supply constants.

All randomness is sourced from Chainlink VRF. The index-based separation between deposits and resolution makes it impossible for any participant, including the contract deployer, to know a day's outcome before staking on it. A paid RNG nudge shifts the next word by +1 without revealing it.
