Trust Model

The contracts are immutably deployed, with no upgrade path or pause switch. All prize pool money must exit as player winnings, under rules defined by publicly verifiable code that can never change. The admin keys do exist, and this page lists everything they can do. None of it reaches your money or your odds.

That was a design requirement, tested against the worst case: someone holding the creator's keys whose only goal is to destroy the game or steal the money could do neither.

The one lever that matters

Randomness comes from Chainlink VRF. If Chainlink ever stops answering, the game needs a way to switch suppliers, and a malicious supplier would control the draws. So the swap requires approval from the game's most committed players, the group with the most to lose from a successful attack: sDGNRS holders. The admin can only propose a new coordinator after 44 hours without a daily random word. Independently of the admin, any holder of 0.5% of player-owned sDGNRS can propose one after 7 days. Either way, the swap executes only when approvals reach a threshold that starts at 50% of player-owned sDGNRS and decays to 5% over seven days, and approvals must also outnumber rejections. The proposal dies the moment the old VRF recovers. Abusing it takes a three-leg parlay: Chainlink fails, someone proposes a hostile coordinator, and sDGNRS holders approve it.

Comps

Every finished craps battle adds 2% of its field's bankroll (~50% of expected losses) to a FLIP comp budget. The admin, or a delegate given an allowance, may spend that budget on free craps battle entries for any player, or add it to an open battle's prize pool. Comps cost exactly what the paid entry burns, can never exceed the budget, and move no ETH.

Everything else is housekeeping

Recover LINK from retired subscriptions After a coordinator swap, may cancel a retired VRF subscription and route its LINK to the live subscription, or to the vault once the game is over. Gated only because the call reaches an address the caller supplies.
Declare a future “thanos” level May flag a level at least three ahead so its entry counts, and those of every level after, divide by up to 256×. A redenomination for runaway demand: it cannot be declared unless 40M+ entries survive the division, and it locks before any ticket for the level exists. Every holder's pot share is unchanged.
Raise a smurf allowance May permanently raise how many smurfs (child accounts a main account opens and funds) a main account can create. The base is zero, and activity score adds one per 120 points. A raise only goes up and never resets the lifetime count. Each smurf costs its owner a full ticket at the current price to open and plays under its own per-account caps, the same as a separate wallet would. No ETH moves and no odds change.
Back liquidations with the vault Off by default. May let the vault buy a liquidated account when sDGNRS cannot fund the quote, at the same price sDGNRS would pay, and set an ETH floor the vault never spends. The vault pays from its own balance; no prize pool is touched.
Sell afKing seats May mint afKing seats once all 1,000 free seats are gone, only while seats in circulation plus active subscriptions stay within 2,000, and may transfer seats the vault holds. A seat only starts a subscription. It carries no odds and no payout.
Run the vault's own positions May play and manage what the vault already holds, the same moves any holder can make with their own assets: advance the game, buy tickets with FLIP, claim winnings, fund the vault's afKing subscription, enter craps battles, deposit and claim coinflips, burn FLIP in the Decimator, and claim the vault's sDGNRS redemptions. May send any token the vault holds except stETH, which backs vault shares, anywhere, including its DGNRS allocation. May unwrap vault DGNRS into soulbound sDGNRS, which votes like any other, capped at 40B over the vault's lifetime.
Manage Lido liquidity May stake game ETH that the reserve calculation treats as excess, and may swap the admin's own ETH for an equal amount of game-held stETH.
Open custom craps battles May create craps battles with custom terms and let other addresses do the same. Nobody is entered automatically; players choose whether to join.
Set NFT presentation May change renderers and colors for deity passes, record trophies and afKing seats, and the icon art until it is finalized. Presentation only, with a built-in fallback.
Curate the charity ballot May fill the 20-address slate. Slots 0–2 lock once filled. sDGNRS holders vote on which address wins.
Post-game GNRUS cleanup After the final sweep, may redeem an inactive holder's GNRUS to that holder. Three years later, may sweep the remaining GNRUS backing to the vault.

Zero rake, disclosed pay

Nobody takes a cut of any wager. Ticket and luckbox ETH goes to the prize pools whole. What the creator gets is fixed in code and public: a deity pass held by the vault, a DGNRS allocation, 80% of presale-box ETH, and the vault's quarter of the staking yield.

Who the protocol trusts

Chainlink for randomness and Lido for yield, the same infrastructure the rest of DeFi stands on, with Ethereum under both. Past that, the only thing left to trust is the code itself, which is public and finished.
