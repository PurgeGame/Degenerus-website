Account Liquidation

A ticket purchase is not refundable. What you can do instead is sell the whole account to a buyer willing to wait, at a price designed to make you think hard about whether you want to.

The price is the point

Only far-future tickets set the price, and the curve gives them a small fraction of face value: 15% for tickets up to 6 levels out, sliding to 10% at 20 levels and 5% at 100. A daily per-account jitter then scales that offer between 70% and 110%, and a second daily draw cuts it to between 40% and 70% of that. In total a quote lands between about 1.4% and 11.6% of the tickets' face value at their target levels, and a very small account can come in lower still. The day's draws are fixed for that account.

Nothing here is a refund. You are selling an account to a buyer at a price you should hate, and the discount is what makes anyone willing to take the other side.

What you get paid in

ETH only. If the game's ETH balance falls short of the price, stETH covers the difference. No replacement tickets and no FLIP are issued. You set a minimum with minEthOut, and the sale reverts if the price comes in under it.

What you give up

The sale hands over the whole account, not just the tickets that set the price. Everything on it goes to the buyer, including claimable ETH, unspent afKing funding and unclaimed rewards, so withdraw or claim first if you want to keep them. Coinflip stakes transfer unchanged. Any afKing subscription on it is cancelled at the next daily afKing run. Selling a main account also hands over every sub account under it, and it destroys the wallet's entire native sDGNRS balance for no payout, so redeem first if you want its value. Wrapped DGNRS is not affected. Deity pass holders cannot liquidate.

Who buys it

Your tickets are not destroyed. They stay in their queues and the account now belongs to the buyer, who receives its jackpot winnings when the target levels arrive. Later awards to the sold account are collected for the buyer, and anyone can trigger that collection, though it pays the caller nothing.

The buyer takes the delay and outcome risk. If neither buyer can meet its funding floor, the sale reverts.

The exact numbers

What counts toward the price

Eligible Tickets still waiting to be printed, 2 to 100 levels beyond the level a ticket would buy into today
Granularity Whole tickets of four entries each. Any leftover entries add nothing to the quote
Adds nothing Near-term tickets, printed tickets and sub accounts. They still go to the buyer with the account
Not allowed Deity pass holders, accounts already sold, the daily draw lock, and everything once the game's ending has triggered, by the death clock or a stalled daily draw

The price is the point

Distance out Offer, before jitter
2–6 levels 15% of face
20 levels 10% of face
100 levels 5% of face

Jitter is 70–110% of the offer, and a second daily draw pays out 40–70% of the jittered amount.

Who buys it

First in line sDGNRS, using its own claimable balance while keeping at least 1 ETH above the price
Backstop The vault, if the vault owner has opted in and can cover the price above its reserve floor
If neither can pay The sale reverts. There is no other buyer
The exit is bad. The position is the point.
