The Jackpots

The game runs in levels. While a level's pool fills, draws pay out every single day. When it fills, the whole pool goes out the door and the next level starts.

A day of draws, playable. Your ticket's four traits against the day's drawn traits: pop the bubbles to reveal prizes.

The level cycle

During the purchase phase, ticket sales fill the level's prize pool. The moment it reaches what the previous level raised, the jackpot phase begins and pays that pool out. So each level has to match the one before it, which is what makes the ladder climb.

After an x00 century level the target resets to 40% of the futurepool, and ticket prices drop back to 0.04 ETH. The ladder begins again from the bottom rather than climbing forever. What does not reset is the century itself: each x00 has to clear a multiple of the previous century's pool, so the cycles keep growing even as the levels inside them get cheap again.

Nothing waits for the level to finish

A level can take days or weeks, but the game does not go quiet while it fills. Every purchase-phase day runs its own draws out of the futurepool, so a ticket bought early is not a bet parked until the level ends. It is working the entire time, and it accumulates more draws the longer the level takes. Slow levels are better for the people already holding tickets, not worse.

Who wins

Winners are ticket holders whose traits match the drawn ones. Each bucket's slice spreads across many winners at descending prize sizes, so a draw is not one person taking everything. There is exactly one exception, the solo bucket, which is a single winner taking the largest share of the day, and it is the reason the finale is worth waiting for.

Base winner counts scale with the ETH budget; the solo slot stays one winner. Which quadrant is solo is random, with gold drawn traits favored when present. The jackpot phase runs three days; a level whose pool fills within a day runs it all in one (turbo). Every x5 level except x95 also runs turbo, and its jackpot day feeds the Decimator.
