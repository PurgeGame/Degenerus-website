Quests & Activity Score

The activity score measures repeated participation, and daily quests are one of its main inputs. Several odds, weighting, and face-value payout curves read it directly; it is money, not a cosmetic XP bar.

The daily quests

Slots 2 per day: an ETH-denominated purchase quest plus a gated random challenge
Reward 100 FLIP per completion
Streak +1 per quest, +5 per level quest; a missed day resets it to zero
Protection Boon shields and a foil-pack floor can absorb missed days
Recycling Winnings rolled back into tickets count the same as fresh ETH

How the score adds up

The score is a sum of components, displayed as points where 100 points equals 1.00:

Source Max points
Quest streak, half a point per streak step; resets with the streak Uncapped component; two daily completions add 1 raw point
Mint streak, 1 per consecutive level played 50
Referred volume, maxed at 25 ETH 50
Lifetime mint count 25
Pass bonuses 10–80
Cashout curse −20 at worst

Passes floor several components, which is why a fresh deity wallet starts at 1.55 and an active whale pass at 1.15. The quest component can keep growing, but total score is hard-capped at 655.34 and the shared reward curves reach their effective cap at 300.00.

The pattern is deliberate. Several products give more engaged players a better contract-defined curve. Two players can buy the same box, pack, or burn and receive different weighting or nominal expected value.
