Quests & Degen Score

Degen score is the game's measure of whether you actually show up. It is not a cosmetic level bar: it is read directly by the odds and payout curves, so it is money.

Showing up is the edge

Most games reward you for spending. This one rewards you for returning. The score is built out of daily actions rather than daily amounts, so a player making small buys every day out-earns a player who arrives once a month with the same total. That is deliberate: the protocol needs a heartbeat more than it needs a whale, and it pays for the heartbeat.

It compounds, and it decays

Streaks are the part that bites. A run of consecutive days is worth more than the same number of scattered days, which means the score you have built is a thing you can lose by getting bored for a week. Passes set a floor under it and a streak shield can absorb one missed day, but nothing makes the score permanent. It is a measure of current engagement, not a trophy for past engagement.

What the score actually buys

The pattern is deliberate. Several products give more engaged players a better contract-defined curve. Two players can buy the same box, pack, or burn and receive different weighting or nominal expected value.

How the score adds up

The score is a sum of components, displayed as points where 100 points equals 1.00:

Source Max points

Cashout curse −20 at worst

Passes floor several components, which is why a fresh deity wallet starts at 1.55 and an active whale pass at 1.15. The quest component can keep growing, but total score is hard-capped at 655.34 and the shared reward curves reach their effective cap at 300.00.
