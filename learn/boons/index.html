Boons

Single-use bonuses that drop from luckbox or get handed to you by a deity. Bonus tickets, bigger coinflip stakes, pass discounts, streak shields. Tied to your wallet, impossible to sell.

Where they come from

There are three sources.

Luckbox. Every ETH luckbox sets aside 10% of its prize budget (capped at 1 ETH) to fund a boon draw. The draw is a real roll, not a guarantee: the chance scales with the box, and the menu is weighted so small boosts land often and the big rolls are rare. The flashiest result is a free whale pass.

Deities. Every deity pass holder gets three boons a day, rolled by VRF from the same weighted menu minus the decimator boosts and deity pass discounts, and their only move is to give them away. The contract will not let a deity boon themselves. A player can receive one deity boon per day, from any deity, and any single deity can give one recipient at most 10 boons over the life of the game. Deity-granted boons expire at the end of the day they were granted.

Protocol draws. The vault and sDGNRS hold deity symbols of their own. Each day each of them awards three boons, drawn among the previous day's ETH Degenerette bettors on its symbol, weighted by stake and degen score.

Why it exists

Boons do three jobs. They put a live discount economy inside luckbox: every box is a shot at cheap tickets or a marked-down pass, funded by the box itself rather than by the prize pools. The short fuses pull decisions forward: a 25% ticket boost with four days on the clock is a reason to buy now, not eventually. And they give deities something no other lottery role has: three favors a day to spend on whoever they want, which turns the game's most expensive pass into a social position with real dealmaking power.
