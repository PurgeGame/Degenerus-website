Boons

Single-use bonuses that drop from lootboxes or get handed to you by a deity pass holder. Bonus tickets, bigger coinflip stakes, pass discounts, streak shields. They're tied to your wallet, they can't be traded, and most of them expire in days. Use them or lose them.

Where they come from

There are exactly two sources.

Lootboxes. Every ETH lootbox sets aside 10% of its prize budget (capped at 1 ETH) to fund a boon draw. The draw is a real roll, not a guarantee: the chance scales with the box, and the menu is weighted so small boosts land often and the big rolls are rare. The flashiest result is a free half whale pass.

Deities. Every deity pass holder gets three boons a day, rolled from the same weighted menu by VRF, and their only move is to give them away. The contract will not let a deity boon themselves. A player can receive one deity boon per day, from any deity, and any single deity can give one recipient at most 10 boons over the life of the game. Deity-granted boons expire at the end of the day they were granted.

The menu

Coinflip boost Your next manual coinflip stake gets 5%, 10%, or 25% extra FLIP added, counted on up to 100,000 FLIP 2 days
Lootbox boost Your next lootbox resolves 5%, 15%, or 25% bigger, counted on up to 10 ETH of box value 2 days
Quest shield Absorbs one missed quest day so your streak survives Until used
Activity bonus Adds 10, 25, or 50 to your mint-count and quest-streak counters, permanent score progress Applied on the spot
Decimator boost Your decimator burn counts 10%, 25%, or 50% bigger, on up to 50,000 FLIP burned Until used; only offered while a decimator is open
Whale pass discount 10%, 20%, or 35% off one whale pass 4 days
Lazy pass discount 10%, 25%, or 50% off a lazy pass, and opens the lazy-pass window even off-schedule 4 days
Deity pass discount 10%, 20%, or 35% off a deity pass; only rolls if passes remain and you don't hold one 4 days
Whale pass Half a whale pass, free: one jackpot entry per level for the next 100 levels Yours to claim

You hold at most one boon per category. A better roll upgrades what you're holding; a worse or equal roll changes nothing.

Why it exists

Boons do three jobs. They put a live discount economy inside lootboxes: every box is a shot at cheap tickets or a marked-down pass, funded by the box itself rather than by the prize pools. The short fuses pull decisions forward: a 25% ticket boost with four days on the clock is a reason to buy now, not eventually. And they give deities something no other lottery role has: three favors a day to spend on whoever they want, which turns the game's most expensive pass into a social position with real dealmaking power.
