WWXRP

Worthless Wrapped XRP. The contract's own documentation reads, in capital letters, “THIS IS A JOKE TOKEN!” The joke has a payout table.

The punchline pays

Luckbox rolls and foil-pack claims pay some prizes as WWXRP Degenerette cards, and that lane is openly rigged in the player's favor: the reel can force an extra match, and its token-denominated expected payout runs from 70% at degen score zero to 130% at the cap. The lane only runs on cards the game hands out; Degenerette bets take ETH or FLIP. Because WWXRP has no protocol backing or redemption path, those percentages are not monetary returns.

The daily burn draw

Burn at least 25 WWXRP to enter that day's draw. Your weight is the whole WWXRP you burned, scaled by your degen score, and you can enter as many times a day as you like. A WWXRP boon adds 4%, 8%, or 12% to one burn's weight; the burn itself is unchanged. Each day rolls two gates: a 1-in-365 BIG prize, and failing that a 1-in-30 SMALL prize. When a gate hits and the winning bucket holds entries, one weighted entrant takes it. BIG pays 100,000 FLIP, SMALL pays 10,000 FLIP, both credited as a coinflip stake rather than a wallet payout. A win has to be claimed within two days of the draw; anyone can submit the claim, and it always pays the winner. An empty winning bucket is a dud day, so the joke token still has to show up to win.

The century incinerator

Every hundredth level triggers a special event. WWXRP burned during a century's x99 jackpot days or x00 purchase phase can turn into FLIP, but only if that century's Big-Ass Flip comes up empty. The rest you find out at level 100.

Why it exists

WWXRP is internal game material for consolation awards and high-variance cards, without a contract promise of backing or redemption. It can still have opportunity cost or a speculative market price; the protocol itself promises neither. The name is a bonus.

The exact numbers

How you end up with it

Source What you get
Losing the daily coinflip 1 WWXRP consolation for the burned FLIP stake
Luckbox rolls A WWXRP Degenerette card on 15% of outcome rolls, staking 5 WWXRP per 0.01 ETH of the roll (at least 1)
Presale boxes A 1-in-10 roll pays a flat 1 WWXRP, whatever the box cost
A skipped Big-Ass Flip A claimable consolation of 1 WWXRP per 1,000 FLIP of your BAF score, at least 1
Foil pack faces 20% of winning claims use the face count as the stake for a WWXRP Degenerette card

Everything the game mints in WWXRP is multiplied by a scale the vault owner sets: 1 at launch, and 0 stops game minting.
