sDGNRS

sDGNRS is the protocol's soulbound stake. You cannot buy it or sell it; you earn it by playing, and the only way to turn it into money is to burn it for a share of what the contract holds.

The protocol-held position plays

The contract itself plays: it holds perpetual tickets rolling a hundred levels deep, subscribes itself to a daily afKing luckbox, and, after its seed period, keeps its FLIP backing on coinflip auto-rebuy.

Getting value out

During a live game, burning sDGNRS joins the open redemption batch. The next VRF request, daily or mid-day, closes the batch and prices all of it at once; the word that answers that request rolls its proportional ETH/stETH base from 21% to 175%. The resolved value normally splits between direct claimable funds and a luckbox, while the FLIP component pays only if the batch's own flip wins, and then rides the next coinflip. Luckbox legs under 0.01 ETH are forfeited back to backing. After GAMEOVER, a burn instead receives its deterministic proportional ETH/stETH share with no FLIP leg. These are contract redemption rules, not a guaranteed investment return.
