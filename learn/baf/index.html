Big Ass Flip

At every x0 transition, a VRF bit decides whether the Big Ass Flip runs. On a win it allocates 10% of the futurepool, or 20% at level 50 and x00, across coinflip leaders and ticket-based samples. On a loss, the pool stays in the futurepool.

The daily coinflip is the qualifying event. Your cumulative winnings on this coin are your BAF score.

How it fires

Leaderboard Cumulative coinflip winnings per player, each 10-level window. Winnings, not volume: losses count for nothing
Trigger Every x0 level, gated on a 50/50 VRF coin flip. Tails, the money stays put and the next BAF draws from a bigger base
Pool 10% of the futurepool; 20% at level 50 and every x00
Payout form Prizes at least 5% of the BAF pool split 50% ETH / 50% lootbox value. Smaller prizes alternate between all ETH and all lootbox value

How the pot splits

Slice Goes to
10% The window's top coinflip winner
5% The biggest single-day flipper of the last 24 hours
5% A random pick from the 3rd and 4th leaderboard slots
10% Two draws among far-future ticket holders: a random slate is sampled, and the top BAF scores in it win
70% Scattered across trait-matched ticket holders over 50 draw rounds, each paying a first and second place

On a normal BAF, scatter samples come from the current and next four levels. At x00, 38 of 50 scatter rounds sample the previous 99 levels. A sampled address still needs positive BAF score to win, and empty or ineligible slots return their share to the futurepool.

Why it exists

The coinflip settles every reward in the game, and BAF makes sustained flipping a competition worth watching. It gives the daily grind a leaderboard while making current and future ticket positions, and past ones at x00, potential sampling routes for active flippers.

Your BAF score is built one flip at a time.

Every FLIP reward you win feeds the same daily flip.
