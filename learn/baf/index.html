Big Ass Flip

Every ten levels the game asks a coin whether to hand a slice of the futurepool to its most persistent flippers. Sometimes it says no, and the slice waits.

The daily coinflip is the qualifying event. Your cumulative winnings on this coin are your BAF score.

Why it exists

The coinflip settles every reward in the game, so everyone ends up flipping whether they meant to or not. BAF turns that background activity into a competition worth watching, and it pays out through ticket positions, which means the people who have been buying ahead are the ones standing in front of it when it fires.

Winnings, not volume

Your BAF score is what you have won on the daily coin, not what you have staked. Losses count for nothing. You cannot buy your way onto the leaderboard by flipping enormous amounts badly; you get there by flipping a lot and running good, which is the only version of this that is worth competing in.

A coin decides whether it fires at all

At each x0 transition the day's communal coinflip decides. A win pays the pool out. On a loss the money stays in the futurepool, the next BAF draws from a bigger base, and every BAF score in the window can be claimed as WWXRP, one per 1,000 FLIP of score. A skipped event is a deferred one, so the cadence is uneven by design and the pot you are competing for is not the same size every time.

The exact numbers

Leaderboard Cumulative coinflip winnings per player, each 10-level window. Winnings, not volume: losses count for nothing
Trigger Every x0 level, gated on that day's communal coinflip. On a loss, the money stays put and the next BAF draws from a bigger base
Pool 10% of the futurepool; 20% at level 50 and every x00
Payout form Prizes at least 5% of the BAF pool split 50% ETH / 50% tickets. Smaller prizes alternate between all ETH and all tickets. A ticket share above 5 ETH comes as whale passes

And how the pot splits:

Slice Goes to
10% The window's top coinflip winner
5% A size-weighted draw over the final purchase day's coinflip deposits
5% A random pick from the 3rd and 4th leaderboard slots
80% Scattered over 48 or more draw rounds: each samples four ticket holders, and the best BAF score among them takes the round's first prize, the second best its second prize (50% and 30% of the pool across all rounds)

Scatter rounds sample four bands equally: the current level, the next level, levels +2 to +5, and levels +6 to +99. Bigger pools run more rounds: 48 below 500 ETH, doubling at each fourfold step, up to 1,536. A sampled address still needs positive BAF score to win, and empty or ineligible slots return their share to the futurepool.

Your BAF score is built one flip at a time.

Every FLIP reward you win feeds the same daily flip.
