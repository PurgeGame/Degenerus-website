Passes

Passes buy exposure to levels that do not exist yet and hand you a degen score you would otherwise spend months earning. They are the shortcut past the grind.

What you are actually buying

Two things at once, and the second is the one people underrate. The obvious purchase is tickets: a pass queues entries across a stretch of levels that have not happened yet, so your money is committed forward instead of riding one draw. The less obvious purchase is a starting degen score. A new wallet begins at zero and climbs slowly; a pass drops you straight onto a floor that would otherwise take months of daily play to reach, and that floor immediately improves your luckbox payouts, Decimator odds, and Degenerette targets.

There are three of them, in escalating order of commitment: lazy covers ten levels, whale covers a hundred, and deity is a permanent soulbound identity that only thirty-two wallets will ever hold.

All three include a 100% mining bonus: earn twice the FLIP mining reward while your pass is active. Lazy and whale passes qualify while their coverage includes the current level; the deity bonus is permanent.

The deity extras

  • Three boons every day, rolled by VRF: purchase discounts, activity boosts, quest-streak shields, and more. You hand them out, capped at 10 lifetime per recipient, and they expire at the end of the day if unused. The contract will not let a deity boon themselves.
  • Never expires, never transfers, a permanent identity rather than an item.
  • Your stored referrer gets the whale-pass ticket allocation and activity floor. The available DGNRS affiliate reserve also pays 0.5% to that referrer, 0.1% to its upline, and 0.05% to the next upline.

The afKing subscription

Spend a seat token to start an afKing subscription: the contract executes one configured ticket or luckbox buy per processed day from your deposited funds or claimable winnings while the balance lasts. Buying a pass mints you a seat, once per account, while the 1,000-seat free tranche lasts. A seat is a transferable token that outlives the pass that earned it, and starting a subscription burns it.
