Passes

Passes prepay future exposure and attach activity-score benefits. Lazy and whale passes queue entries over fixed level ranges; deity passes create permanent virtual entries and a soulbound game identity. A valid pass can also unlock the afKing daily purchase subscription.

A valid one-use boon can discount a pass purchase. Lazy passes are normally available at levels 0–2 and eligible x9/x0 checkpoints, or through a boon; renewal opens when 7 or fewer covered levels remain.

The deity extras

  • Three boons every day, rolled by VRF: purchase discounts, activity boosts, quest-streak shields, and more. You hand them out, capped at 10 lifetime per recipient, and they expire at the end of the day if unused. The contract will not let a deity boon themselves.
  • Never expires, never transfers, a permanent identity rather than an item.
  • Your stored referrer gets the whale-pass ticket allocation and activity floor. The available DGNRS affiliate reserve also pays 0.5% to that referrer, 0.1% to its upline, and 0.05% to the next upline.

The afKing subscription

Hold a valid pass and you can subscribe to afKing: the contract executes one configured ticket or lootbox buy per processed day from your deposited funds or claimable winnings while the balance lasts.
