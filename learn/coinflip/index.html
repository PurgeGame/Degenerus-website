The Coinflip

Nearly every FLIP the protocol hands out arrives as a stake in one daily coinflip, and the whole game shares a single outcome. Win together, lose together.

One flip, everybody

The outcome is communal: a single bit of the day's VRF word decides every stake in the game, and each player's entire day, awards and manual stakes alike, rides that one bit. Total FLIP supply moves in lumps because of it: a loss day burns the game's staked float at once, while a win day expands it according to the payout table. Other reward paths add supply separately.

Why it exists

The flip is a filter: issued FLIP usually takes coinflip risk before it becomes claimable, limiting passive reward streams.

One more thing: cash out winnings after days of inactivity and the protocol may curse you, docking degen score until someone pays 100 FLIP to lift it, or you buy a ticket. It is mostly a joke. Mostly.

Let it ride

Winnings become claimable FLIP, or arm auto-rebuy and the contract rolls them into the next day's flip. Set a take-profit amount and every whole multiple of it banks to your balance on a win, leaving only the remainder to ride: set 1,000, win 2,500, and 2,000 is yours while 500 rolls. Rolled stakes earn a 0.75% bonus. One loss ends the run.
