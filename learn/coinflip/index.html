The Coinflip

Most protocol-issued FLIP, including quests, affiliate commissions, and purchase bonuses, enters as a stake in a daily communal coinflip. Win and the stake returns with a variable bonus. Lose and it burns.

The daily flip

Odds True 50/50, decided once daily for everyone at once: the whole game wins together or loses together
Loss Stake burns, 1 WWXRP consolation
Bonus days Sweetened multipliers at level 0, each level's first jackpot day, and the post-BAF day on x0 levels

Those multipliers are conditional on winning. Including the 50% loss outcome, an ordinary manual stake has slightly below 100% nominal expected return. Bonus days can push it higher.

Let it ride

Winnings become claimable FLIP, or arm auto-rebuy and the contract rolls them into the next day's flip. Set a take-profit amount and every whole multiple of it banks to your balance on a win, leaving only the remainder to ride: set 1,000, win 2,500, and 2,000 is yours while 500 rolls. Rolled stakes earn a 0.75% bonus, capped at 1,000 FLIP per recycle. One loss ends the run.

One flip, everybody

The outcome is communal: a single bit of the day's VRF word decides every stake in the game, and each player's entire day, awards and manual stakes alike, rides that one bit. Total FLIP supply moves in lumps because of it: a loss day burns the game's staked float at once, while a win day expands it according to the payout table. Other reward paths add supply separately.

Why it exists

The flip is a filter: issued FLIP usually takes coinflip risk before it becomes claimable, limiting passive reward streams.

One more thing: cash out winnings after days of inactivity and the protocol may curse you, docking activity score until someone pays 100 FLIP to lift it, or you buy a ticket. It is mostly a joke. Mostly.
