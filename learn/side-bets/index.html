The Side Bets

A parimutuel book lets you bet on the game's own trajectory instead of on a draw. One fixed bet per address per round, OVER or UNDER, and the winning side splits everything the losing side put up.

The book

Every level banks the prize pool it actually achieved when it ends, and that number is written once and never revised. A round compares two of those steps: the growth the next level achieves over this one, against the growth this level achieved over the one before it. Faster is OVER. Ties are UNDER.

It is a bet on acceleration, not on size. A level can bank a bigger pool than its predecessor and still settle UNDER, because what is scored is the rate.

Betting is open during the jackpot phase, including while a day's random word is in flight, and the phase's final draw closes it. A turbo phase pays out in a single draw and has no betting window.

Placing the bet also completes a once-per-level betting quest, worth 150 FLIP until the second jackpot draw and 37 FLIP after it. It moves your quest streak by 1. The FLIP needs the level quest's own gate, a whole ticket for this level plus either a five-level streak or any pass ever held, or an active afKing subscription. Without that you can still bet, you just bet for nothing extra.

Rounds at a century boundary are lopsided and known to be: the pool arithmetic at x99, x00 and x01 is not the ordinary step. Price accordingly.

Why the reward drops

A parimutuel book pays the last mover best, because the final bettor sees the whole board before committing. The book answers that with a quest reward that shrinks as the phase runs rather than with a cutoff. Bet early into a thin book and you are guessing, but you are paid more for it.

Getting paid

The game pushes each round's answer the instant its terms are final, and the answer is stored as a bit, so a payout never re-derives anything. Nobody claims. The game's settlement stage, which runs inside the mineFlip() call, walks each sealed round's winners and credits every one of them automatically as coinflip credit.

Who can bet

Any wallet that has ever bought anything. One bet per address per round, with no amount to choose and no averaging in, which is what makes committing early against a thin book a real decision instead of a mechanical one.

What a win pays

The stake burns the moment you place it. Every bet is the same size, so each side is a count rather than a pot, and the payout is the whole book divided by the winners:

Book OVER wins UNDER wins
4 OVER, 1 UNDER 1,250 FLIP each 5,000 FLIP
3 OVER, 3 UNDER 2,000 FLIP each 2,000 FLIP each

If nobody took the losing side, winners get their 1,000 FLIP back and nothing more. If nobody took the winning side, the stakes stay burned. Payouts arrive as coinflip credit, so they take the daily 50/50 like other issued FLIP.

The book opens with the jackpot phase.
