The Biggest Bounties

Five all-time records, one growing pot, and a soulbound trophy that follows whoever currently holds the mark. The bar only ever goes up.

Five records, one pot

The protocol keeps a permanent record of the biggest single coinflip deposit, the biggest Degenerette bet, the biggest luckbox deposit, the biggest ticket buy anybody has ever made, and the biggest scheduled craps run. All five are backed by the same FLIP pool, which grows every day whether or not anyone is chasing it. Five separate competitions, one shared prize.

Taking the mark and taking the money are different things

This is the part that catches people out. Beating the standing record by any margin makes you the record holder and moves the trophy to your wallet. Getting paid is a higher bar: your number has to clear the standing mark by a fifth. A run of narrow beats can pass the trophy around for months without a single wei leaving the pool. The dice run is the exception: any new high point claims.

A dormant record is worth more than a busy one

A claim does not empty the pot. It takes a share that starts at 5% and grows by half a percent for every day that category has gone unclaimed, up to 75%. Each of the five keeps its own clock, and only an actual claim resets it, so a record nobody has been able to beat becomes progressively more valuable to whoever finally breaks it. The quiet categories are the ones worth hunting.

The trophy

Each record carries a soulbound NFT called The BIGGEST Degenerus, minted once and force-moved by the contract to each new mark holder. You never claim it and you can never sell it or transfer it. Its metadata is the size of your record and the number of days you have held it, so it reads as a running scoreboard rather than a collectible. Losing the record loses the trophy the same block.

Why it exists

Almost everything else in the game rewards consistency. This rewards the single largest thing you have ever done, permanently, with no reset at the end of a level or a cycle. It is the one leaderboard where a whale who shows up once and swings enormous leaves a mark that outlasts them, and the growing pot means the game is always quietly offering money to whoever wants to try.

The pool and the claim

Starts at 10,000 FLIP
Grows by 2,000 FLIP per day, plus 0.2% of each completed level's prize pool
To hold the record Beat the standing mark by any amount
To claim the pool Beat the standing mark by a fifth (dice run: any new high)
Claim pays 5% of the pool, +0.5% per day that category has gone unclaimed, capped at 75% (reached 140 days after a claim)
Also pays 0.01% to 0.15% of the sDGNRS reward pool, scaling with the accrued share
Trophy Soulbound NFT per record, moved automatically on every new mark

A record beaten without clearing the claim bar ratchets the mark and moves the trophy but pays nothing, and it does not reset that category's clock. FLIP-redeemed ticket buys route through redemption rather than the purchase path, so they never arm the ticket record.

Records are set with size. The pot is won with patience.

Every category grows until somebody clears the bar by a fifth.
