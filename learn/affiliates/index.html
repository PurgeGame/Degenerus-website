The Affiliate Deal

Bring people in and the protocol pays you out of freshly minted FLIP, not out of their deposit. Your referral's money still goes entirely into the pools.

How it works

A link supplies an on-chain code. The first purchase or registration stores the referral permanently. A blank, invalid, or self-referral code on that first purchase locks the account to no referrer, and its commission pots go by coin toss to the vault or sDGNRS.

For an ordinary eligible purchase, the contract converts the purchase basis into FLIP units and applies the rate below. That creates a nominal commission pot without removing ETH from the purchase's pool routing. A code owner may also set a 0–25% kickback, which is returned to the buyer from that pot.

Who receives an ordinary pot

After any kickback, a deterministic, publicly knowable daily roll sends the entire purchase pot to one address: the direct affiliate with 75% weight, its upline with 20%, or the next upline with 5%. Those are probabilities, not a three-way split. The winner receives a FLIP credit for the next coinflip, so the credited face amount is not a guaranteed realized payout.

afKing is different: its accrued 7% base is divided 75%/20%/5% when claimed, without a roll. Its recipients still receive FLIP credit. A Degenerette referral reward goes directly to the player's stored referrer.

The deity exception

A deity purchase gives the buyer's stored referrer the associated whale-pass entries and stats. It also draws DGNRS from the available affiliate reserve: 0.5% to the direct referrer, 0.1% to the first upline, and 0.05% to the second. Previously reserved level claims are excluded from that available balance.

DGNRS and activity

DGNRS is a protocol token, not legal equity. Its initial allocation reserves 30% for affiliate rewards. At each level transition, the top affiliate receives 0.5% of the then-current affiliate pool; 2.5% of the remainder is snapshotted for score-proportional claims by qualifying affiliates. Unclaimed value remains in the pool.

Affiliate score from the previous five levels can also add up to +0.50 to the referrer's degen score. The cap is reached at 25 ETH of contract-weighted referred volume; recycled and early-level volume do not carry identical weights.

Why it exists

Affiliates supply player acquisition and attention that code cannot create. The current rates and routing are in the contracts, but realized value still depends on coinflip outcomes, continued game activity, and token value.
