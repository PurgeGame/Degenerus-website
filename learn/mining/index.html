Mining FLIP

The game has no server and no team pressing buttons. Every day it needs somebody to send a transaction, and it pays FLIP to whoever does.

Somebody has to press the button

An on-chain game does not run on its own. Advancing the day, paying the jackpots, rolling a level over, opening the queued boxes and settling Degenerette bets: each of those is a transaction that some address has to broadcast and pay gas for. Most protocols hide this behind a company running a bot. Degenerus puts it in the open and pays a bounty, so the thing keeping the game alive is an incentive rather than a promise.

Anyone can call it, and anyone is paid

Calling mineFlip() is permissionless, and so is the bounty. There is no list of eligible callers, no ticket you must have bought and no pass you must hold. If the game needs cranking and you crank it, you are paid for the gas the call used.

An active pass doubles the reward. A deity pass always counts, and lazy and whale passes count while their coverage includes the current level. The reward also doubles when the daily RNG lock was already on at the start of your call, and the two stack.

It pays a share of your gas, not a profit

The bounty is pegged to the gas the call used, not to a yield target. The first 1,000,000 gas of every call is unpaid, and the rest is reimbursed at the block's base fee, capped at 0.5 gwei, times 30%. With a pass that is 60%, and in either case the reward only exists as FLIP coinflip credit, so it still has to survive the daily 50/50 like every other FLIP the protocol issues. This is deliberate. Mining is meant to be liveness work that somebody is always willing to do, not a farm worth building an operation around.

The longer it sits, the more it pays

The clock starts at the latest randomness request or at the 22:57 UTC day reset, whichever is later. Every 30 minutes without a crank raises both the base fee cap and the share of gas reimbursed, up to four steps: 30% at the start, then 75%, 120%, 165% and 210%, with the cap doubling from 0.5 gwei to 1, 2, 4 and 8. The game gets more insistent the longer it is ignored, which means the scenario where nobody can be bothered is also the scenario where cranking pays best.

You cannot farm it by splitting the work

Every call forfeits its first million gas. Slicing the work into many small calls only forfeits another unpaid million per call, so the honest full-batch crank is the only version that pays well.

The exact numbers

Paid in FLIP, credited as a coinflip stake, converted at the current ticket price
Reward (gas used − 1,000,000) × the lower of the block base fee and the cap × the share below
Stall ladder Start: 0.5 gwei cap, 30%. After 30 minutes: 1 gwei, 75%. After 1 hour: 2 gwei, 120%. After 90 minutes: 4 gwei, 165%. After 2 hours: 8 gwei, 210%
Active pass Share doubled: deity always, lazy and whale while their coverage includes the current level
Daily RNG lock Share doubled when the lock was on at the start of the call; stacks with a pass

The day resets at 22:57 UTC. Growth side-bet settlement is one of the stages a mineFlip() call runs, and its gas pays like any other. The crank pays nothing once the game is over.

The game pays its own electricity bill.

No server, no team, no scheduled job. Just a bounty that goes up until somebody takes it.
