Purchase options

The Buy panel has three choices. Use any one of them, or combine them in a single ETH transaction. The total shown on Buy includes every option you selected.

Tickets
The standard play. Each whole ticket has four random traits and four jackpot entries. Fractional tickets are allowed. Read the full ticket guide →
Lootbox size
Add an ETH-sized box for a later randomized reveal. Results can include tickets, tokens, or spins; the outcome is not a guaranteed cash return. Read the lootbox guide →
Foil pack
A one-per-level add-on costing ten ticket prices. It contains four rarity-boosted tickets that are re-graded during the level. Read the foil guide →

At checkout

  • Wallet and Claimable choose which ETH source is spent first.
  • Any purchase FLIP bonus appears directly below Buy before you confirm.
  • When Mint with FLIP is available, it buys tickets only; lootboxes and foil packs are disabled.

Ticket traits, lootbox results, and foil traits all depend on future randomness. Buying does not guarantee a winning ticket or a profitable reveal.

Pick what you want, then check the total on Buy.
