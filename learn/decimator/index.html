The Decimator

Burn FLIP to enter a craps battle for a slice of the futurepool. Your degen score multiplies what your burn is worth, and burning early beats burning late.

Why it exists

FLIP has two very different sinks. During the redemption window, 1,000 FLIP buys one ticket for any wallet at all. That is the flat exit: known quantity, known price, no opinion required. A Decimator burn trades the token for a chance at ETH instead, and the chance is not the same for everyone. It can pay more, pay less, or pay nothing. Either way the FLIP is gone, which is the point of the sink.

The battle

Each burn builds a credited stack. At resolution a random half of the entries, at most 1,000, each play one craps run on a shared set of dice. You pick the board when you burn: up to seven chips on the lines you choose, with the dice scattering the rest of ten. Every run rolls the same dice, so boards compete on equal terms. A run ends when it busts, after 48 shooters, or at 511 rolls. Your score is your credited stack times the peak bankroll the run reached, so a big stack on a board that never gets hot loses to a modest stack on one that does.

Entries outside the draw score nothing. The highest scores among the runs take the places.

Your stack is your engagement

Your degen score multiplies every burn, from 1.0x at a score of 0 to 1.705x at 2.35, 1.9x at 5.00 and 2.0x at 300.00. The multiplier is read at the moment you burn, so burning again at a higher score credits that burn at the higher rate. A player who has been showing up daily is buying into a materially better game than one who arrived this morning, out of the same pool, with the same token.

Early conviction is priced

The entry window opens a level before the milestone fires, and when you burn inside it matters. A burn placed on the day the window opens is credited in full. Each day after that, a burn is credited at 90% of the day before: 0.9x on the second day, 0.81x on the third, and so on. The Decimator pays for the opinion you formed before the evidence arrived.

The exact numbers

Fires at x05, x15, …, x85 of each cycle (not x95), plus every x00 century
Entry window Opens when level x4 (or x99 before a century) meets its target and closes when the milestone level meets its own
Entry Burn FLIP, 2,000 minimum. A later burn adds to the same entry, and the board from the last burn counts. sDGNRS enters automatically on the opening day
Pool 10% of the futurepool, 30% at x00
Credited stack Burn × 0.9 per day since the window opened × degen score multiplier
Degen score multiplier 1.0x at 0, 1.705x at 2.35, 1.9x at 5.00, 2.0x at 300.00
Score Credited stack × the peak bankroll your run reaches
Who plays A random half of the entries, at most 1,000; only they can place
Places Entries ÷ 10 rounded up, at least 20, at most 200, and never more than half the entries
Payout 5% of the pool is held back and the rest splits into equal shares, one per place. The champion takes the held-back 5% on top of an equal share
Half whale passes Half of the champion's payout, rounded down to whole half whale passes at 2.25 ETH each, is paid as passes and the rest as ETH. Once an equal share is 2.25 ETH or more, the places below the champion alternate: second place ETH, third place whole half passes, fourth ETH, and so on. The ETH that buys passes goes back to the futurepool
No places With no entries, or a single entry, nobody wins and the pool stays in the futurepool

At the x05 to x85 levels, the level's jackpot-phase ETH draw also funds generated entries, up to as many as there are real ones. They are played for random jackpot ticket holders at the average stack, add to the pool, and take places like any other entry.
