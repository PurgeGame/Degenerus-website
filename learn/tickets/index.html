Tickets

A ticket is the basic unit of play. Every ticket carries four random traits and four entries into the level's jackpot draws. 100% of ticket ETH routes to game prize pools; no purchase-time share goes to an operator or team.

What you get

Each ticket rolls four traits, one per quadrant, and each trait is a symbol plus a color. The jackpot draws pay trait-matched holders, and rarer matches split the prize with fewer people. One ticket counts as four jackpot entries; you can buy fractions, multiples, and future levels ahead of time, which is how passes work under the hood. Future-level tickets stay locked in until their level's jackpot resolves; Salvage Swap lets you sell an unresolved position back if you change your mind.

Two tickets as minted. The quadrants always draw from the same four families: crypto, zodiac, cards, dice. The left ticket's Bitcoin rolled gold, the ~1-in-128 color.

Symbol and color roll independently, so a quadrant has 64 possible traits, weighted heavily toward the common colors. Gold is 32x rarer than pink, purple, or green.

What a ticket costs

Levels Price per ticket
0–4 0.01 ETH
5–9 0.02 ETH
x01–x29 of each 100-level cycle 0.04 ETH
x30–x59 0.08 ETH
x60–x89 0.12 ETH
x90–x99 0.16 ETH
x00 century levels 0.24 ETH

The ladder climbs through each 100-level cycle and then resets: a ticket at level 101 costs 0.04 ETH, the same as one at level 11. Price alone does not set the size of the prize. A level's pool target is whatever the previous level raised, except for the target reset after each x00 century level. During a level's redemption window, which opens once the pool hits its target and runs through the jackpot days, 1,000 FLIP buys one ticket instead of paying ETH.

Where the money goes

Prize pool for the level being entered 90%
Futurepool, backing daily prizes and future levels 10%
Operator, house, team 0%

Affiliate commissions are minted as FLIP on top of the purchase, not carved out of it.

Every ticket mints you FLIP

Every ticket bought with ETH or winnings credits the buyer 100 FLIP, a tenth of the ticket's FLIP face value, on top of the jackpot entries. Two bonuses stack on the base:

Base, every ticket 100 FLIP
Recycled winnings (3+ tickets' worth of claimable in one buy) +100 FLIP per ticket's worth recycled
Bulk buy (10+ tickets) +50 FLIP per ticket

A qualifying recycled bulk buy can credit 250 FLIP per ticket, a quarter of face value. Credits arrive as a coinflip stake, so they ~double or die on the daily flip before they are yours to spend. FLIP-paid tickets mint no FLIP back.
