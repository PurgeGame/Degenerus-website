Degenerette

The slot machine. Bet ETH to win ETH or FLIP to win FLIP, against a payout table that tops out above 100,000x. Casinos bury their par sheets. This one is published in the contract.

The par sheet is public

Every slot machine ever built has a par sheet: the table that says what each outcome pays and how often it comes up. Casinos treat theirs as a trade secret, because a player who can read it can price the machine. This one is a constant in a contract you can read from your own node. The whole edge, such as it is, is published.

How a card scores

Each card reveals four quadrants, each with a symbol and a color. Every symbol match scores a point, hero included. Colors score on their own: equal colors score a point, and a wild matches any color. Your hero quadrant is always wild, and the house rolls a wild in any quadrant one time in sixteen. A wild against a wild scores two. That gives every card a score from 1 to 9, and anything from 3 up pays. Each wild on the house card adds 25% to the payout. The scoring is why the top of the table is so steep: stacking symbol hits is ordinary, but stacking symbol and color hits across four quadrants is not.

Playing more makes the machine pay better

The payout target is not a constant. It reads your degen score, so the same card at the same stake is worth more to a player who has been showing up than to one who has not. This is the opposite of how a casino treats a regular: the comps here are in the math rather than the buffet.

The hero symbol reaches outside the machine

Each day one symbol is named hero, and the odds of a symbol getting picked scale with how much ETH was wagered on it in Degenerette the day before. The hero symbol is then locked into one quadrant of the day's jackpot draw. So Degenerette betting is not sealed off from the rest of the game: it leans on which traits win the jackpots. Only one quadrant, only the symbol and not the color, and you cannot choose the traits on tickets you already hold, so it is a real lever and a small one.

Payouts are multiples of the stake with no house wilds, from degen score 0 to the effective cap. Each wild on the house card adds 25%. A score of 9 always needs a house wild on your hero quadrant, so it pays at least 1.25x its table value. Scores 1 and 2 pay nothing.

The ETH lane adds 5 percentage points of expected prize value, concentrated in score 6–9. Large ETH results partly recirculate through luckbox, and total ETH paid is capped against the futurepool, so a figure above 100% is not a promise of cash profit. WWXRP percentages are denominated in a token with no backing or redemption value.
