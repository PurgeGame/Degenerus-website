The slot machine. Bet ETH to win ETH, FLIP to win FLIP, or WWXRP to win WWXRP, against a payout table published in the contract that tops out above 100,000x. Casinos bury their par sheets. This one's public, because it has no choice.

How a spin scores

A spin rolls four quadrants, each with a symbol and a color. Every symbol match scores a point, the hero quadrant's symbol counts double, and a color only scores if its symbol hit too. That gives every spin a score from 0 to 9, and anything from 2 up pays:

Score Payout
2 ~1.95–2.81x
3 ~4.87–7.02x
4 ~15–22x
5 ~44–63x
6 ~200–288x
7 ~1,000–1,500x
8 ~51,000–74,000x
9 ~107,000–209,000x

FLIP-staked wins ride one more 50/50, double or nothing, before they mint. Details below.

Ranges depend on hero color and the roll. The honest ETH/FLIP base tables are calibrated toward a 100% nominal expected payout; the activity curve climbs from 90% toward that ceiling and tops out just under it. Individual spins remain highly volatile.

The ETH lane adds 5 percentage points of expected prize value, concentrated in score 6–9. Large ETH results partly recirculate through lootboxes, and total ETH paid is capped against the futurepool, so a figure above 100% is not a promise of cash profit. WWXRP percentages are denominated in a token with no backing or redemption value.
