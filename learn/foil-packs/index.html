Foil Packs

The premium pack. Ten tickets' worth of ETH buys four rarity-boosted tickets, and every one of them gets a fresh shot at a prize ladder each day from its resolve day until the level ends. One pack per level, per wallet.

The pack

Price 10x the current ticket price
Contents 4 tickets, real jackpot entries like any others
Color rarity boost 2x at zero activity score, ~5x for a daily player, 6x curve cap
Limit One pack per level per wallet
Pool split 75% current prize pool, 25% futurepool
Bonus Floors your quest streak at 12; pays standard affiliate rates

The traits roll from a future day's randomness, unknowable to anyone at purchase time, including the protocol. Rarer colors mean rarer trait matches, and rarer matches split jackpot prizes with fewer people.

The daily re-grade

Each foil ticket is graded against both winning trait sets published each day, from its resolve day until the level completes. A symbol match scores 1; matching symbol and color scores 2. Across four quadrants, each comparison grades from 0 to 8. A slow level therefore creates more comparisons for the same pack.

Faces are a spin stake, not a direct payout. The spin can lose.

Faces set the stake for an isolated Degenerette spin: 40% ETH at one ticket price per face, 40% FLIP at 1,000 per face, or 20% WWXRP at 1 per face. The spin can lose or win; faces are not a direct payout. Grade 8 also grants half a whale pass.

The boost runs on your activity score.

A zero-score wallet gets 2x rarity. A daily player gets 5x. Same price.
