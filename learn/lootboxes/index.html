Luckbox

A luckbox is an ETH-denominated box whose contents are decided by a random word that does not exist yet. How much it is worth to open depends on how much you play.

Nobody knows what is in it, including the protocol

A luckbox is sealed against a random word that has not been generated yet. At the moment you buy, the contents are not merely hidden from you, they do not exist. There is no seed to leak, no ordering to game, and nothing for the protocol to know that you do not. The box is decided later, by Chainlink, for everyone at once.

What's inside

After the boon set-aside, each box resolves as one outcome roll. Ticket prizes generally target upcoming levels rather than paying withdrawable ETH.

A tenth of the box's prize budget (capped at 1 ETH) funds a side boon draw; the deposited ETH itself still routes exactly as the table below shows. A boon is a single-use bonus like free tickets on your next buy, a pass discount, or a quest-streak shield.

The payout curve is public

The whole curve is a constant in the contract, so the box is one of the few gambling products anywhere whose house edge you can read before you play. It is also the product where engagement pays most directly: the multiplier runs from 90% of the box's value at zero activity to 145% at the top, which means the same box is a losing purchase for a tourist and a winning one for a regular.

Two limits shape that. Your score is frozen into each box when you buy it, so you cannot grind the score up after the fact, and the portion above the neutral 100% applies to at most 10 ETH of luckbox deposits per player per level. The bonus is real, and it is capped.
