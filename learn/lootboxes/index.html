Lootboxes

A lootbox is an ETH-denominated box, revealed from a future random word. The contract applies an activity-based multiplier to its prize budget: 90% at score zero, 100% at 0.60, and up to 145%. That multiplier is not a guaranteed cash return; outcomes are volatile and often pay tickets, tokens, or spins.

What's inside

Chance Prize
40% Tickets, with a variance roll from about 0.6x the box's value up to 11x, or 19x when the roll lands on far-future levels
15% DGNRS, drawn from the lootbox allocation of the protocol token
15% FLIP

10% Three FLIP Degenerette spins

After the boon set-aside, a multiplier-adjusted box above 0.5 ETH splits into two independent outcome rolls. Ticket prizes generally target upcoming levels rather than paying withdrawable ETH.

A tenth of the box's prize budget (capped at 1 ETH) funds a side boon draw; the deposited ETH itself still routes exactly as the table above shows. A boon is a single-use bonus like free tickets on your next buy, a pass discount, or a quest-streak shield.

The score on the first deposit into a pending box is frozen for that box. The portion above the neutral 100% multiplier applies to at most 10 ETH of lootbox deposits per player per level. A higher multiplier improves the nominal prize budget, but it does not remove outcome variance, token-price risk, or the delay on future tickets.

Where the money goes

Futurepool, backing upcoming levels and daily prizes 90%
Pool for the level being filled 10%

That is the standard split.
