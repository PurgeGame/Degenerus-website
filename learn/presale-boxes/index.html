Presale Boxes

A capped early-sale mechanic. Spend on the game while the window is open and you earn credit toward a box; the earliest boxes are the richest ones anybody will ever open.

You cannot just buy one

Box capacity is earned rather than purchased. Every ETH spent on tickets, luckboxes, foil packs and passes while the window is open earns a quarter of its value in box credit, and the credit is what buys the box. Taking the whole 50 ETH tranche means putting 200 ETH into the game itself.

Earliest is richest

The DGNRS inside is front-loaded, so the boxes bought at the start of the window hold materially more than the ones bought at the end. The schedule is public before the window opens.

Where the money goes

If the full 50 ETH sells, 40 ETH routes to the vault and 10 ETH to sDGNRS claimable backing. The Vault's separate DGNRS allocation vests through level 30, which is the mechanism that keeps the Vault's owner holding an interest in the game still being alive at level 30.
