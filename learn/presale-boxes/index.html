Presale Boxes

Presale boxes are a capped early-sale mechanic. While the window is open, eligible ticket, lootbox, and pass purchase value earns box credit; the wallet spends that credit 1:1 to buy a box. Cumulative box volume stops at 50 ETH, and that cap is the only closing condition; there is no time or level cutoff.

The deal

Access Box credit only: 25% of eligible ticket, lootbox, lazy-pass, whale-pass, and deity-pass purchase value while the window is open
Minimum box 0.01 ETH
Lifetime cap 50 ETH, then closed forever
Contents 50% FLIP / 40% DGNRS / 10% WWXRP, selected by a verifiable random roll
DGNRS curve The first 10 ETH of boxes pay 3x the base DGNRS rate, stepping down to 1x by the final tier
The closing box Whoever crosses the 50 ETH cap sweeps every DGNRS left in the presale pool
Proceeds 80% vault, 20% sDGNRS

Why it exists

If all 50 ETH sells, 40 ETH routes to the vault and 10 ETH to sDGNRS claimable backing. The creator's separate DGNRS allocation vests through level 30. The credit gate ensures box capacity is earned through prior game spend; it does not make a box profitable.

The credit comes from playing.

Tickets, lootboxes, and pass purchases earn it while the window is open.
