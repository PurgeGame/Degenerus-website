Degenerus Protocol · testnet run #68 · Base Sepolia · drawn to scale from on-chain data

Three levels finished with nobody buying. The fourth ran out of clock.

Buying stopped 444 blocks into Level 38 and never resumed. The last 735.2 ETH of purchases cleared Level 38. Levels 39, 40, and 41 then reached their targets with nobody buying, after 8, 13, and 30 game days, each slower than the last. From day 46 through day 137 the game credited ticketholders 5,790.3 ETH in jackpot awards. Then the reserve ran thin. Level 42 opened on day 107, 2,076.1 ETH short of a 2,768.9 ETH target, climbed for 30 days, and stopped 780.9 ETH short. On day 138 the game latched GAMEOVER and paid its entire remaining 3,454.9 ETH to 65 wallets in a single transaction. This is all 137 days of it.

Observed · levels 1 → 42 · days 2 → 137

Thirty-eight levels bought, three levels without a buyer, one level short

Each bar is a level: the pool it banked, the reserve behind it on the day it opened, and the insurance on top. The banked pool is levelPrizePool[L], written once when the level completes and never touched again, so the ladder only ever goes up: 225.9, 231.3, 253.4, then on to 2,589.0, 2,713.6, 2,721.7, and 2,768.9. The gold tick is the ratchet that level had to clear, which is simply the previous level's bar. The number under each bar is the days from that level opening to the next one. Levels came every 1 to 4 days while people were buying. Level 38 took 5, because its jackpot ran after the last purchase. Then they came every 10, 15, and 32 days with nobody buying, and the last one never came.

The reserve is the whole story: it peaks at 6,236.5 ETH on day 45, and falls with every level after that.The futurepool stood at 6,226.6 when Level 39 opened, 4,799.1 for Level 40, 3,415.5 for Level 41, and 2,454.6 for Level 42. By day 137 it held 732.7.

Observed · days 2 → 137 · every ETH the contract holds

The pools shrink after the last buyers, the total does not

The ladder counts pools only. This is the same run counting everything the contract holds: the claimable winnings credited to players and not yet claimed, the nextpool, the futurepool, and the insurance. The stack always adds to the contract's balance. After the last purchases the total barely moves: ETH leaves the pools as awards are credited and stays in the contract as claimable winnings. The total rose 739.7 from day 45 to day 50, 735.2 of it the last purchases, and 114.0 from day 50 to day 137. The contract's ETH balance did not move in that second stretch. The growth is its stETH balance.

Total ETH held never fell after the last purchases: 16,841.7 on day 47, 16,844.5 on day 50, 16,958.5 on day 137.What fell is everything outside claimable winnings, from 8,224.8 on day 50 to 3,454.3 on day 137, while claimable winnings rose from 8,619.7 to 13,504.2. GAMEOVER pays out the first number and leaves the second claimable.

After the last buyers: three levels clear, the fourth fails, and the game pays out what is left

The whole stretch in one frame. The first bar is Level 38 as it opens, and the last purchases of the run arrive in the next 444 blocks, so the second bar already holds them and is taller than the first. The dashed box above a stack is the net ETH that left the pools since the previous bar, so a bar's solid part plus its box reaches the top of the previous bar's solid part. The figure under each total compares that bar with the same point one level earlier, named by its day. Each level's ratchet is drawn only across the window it governs, because it steps up every time. Observed · game over · day 138 · block 47,944,594

It pays out everything it has left, in one transaction

GAMEOVER pays the pot out. The available balance, everything outside existing claims, is drained into a terminal jackpot for the cohort holding tickets at Level 42, the level that never filled.

65 wallets were paid, and one of them took 2,072.94 ETH of the 3,454.9.The largest single award was 2,072.94 ETH. The nextpool, futurepool, and current pool all read 0 afterwards, and 13,504.2 ETH of already credited winnings stayed claimable on top.

Provenance. Every figure is measured state, with no projections and no models. Pool bars come from the daily prize-pool snapshots of the Run 68 database. The drain is the terminal block's ETH jackpot awards, block 47,944,594. Values are display ETH, raw testnet wei ÷ 10¹², the run dashboard's normalization to mainnet ticket prices. A game day lasted 20 minutes in this run. Pool figures are the contract's own PrizePoolDailySnapshot events, the settled pools when each game day is sealed. The database has no snapshot for day 46, so awards credited that day are counted with the next one. The insurance bar is the game's available balance outside the current, next, and future pools, which holds retained insurance and yield. The last day of Level 42's purchase phase is day 137, and GAMEOVER processed on day 138.

The ladder is read from chain, not inferred. Each level's banked pool is levelPrizePool[L], read as an eth_call to prizePoolTargetView() at the block that completed the level. That call returns the target of level L+1, which is exactly the pool level L banked, and the contract writes it once, at the level's transition. The Level 41 read, 2,768.9, is the target Level 42 had to reach, and it matches the target view recorded at the final level's opening and closing blocks.

What counts as a payout. Awards are the ETH jackpot awards credited between two daily snapshots, summed from recorded events. Ticket and pass awards are excluded, and so is the Level 40 BAF draw, which pays BAF winners out of the futurepool and is listed separately. The total is gross: winnings can be recycled into further play, and a credited prize need not have been withdrawn. It does not measure player profit. The terminal award sum leaves 115 original wei of rounding dust.

ETH in. Purchases are read from the chain, not from the indexed ETH-in table. 208 ETH-bearing transactions sent 735.2 ETH to the game between blocks 47,888,791 and 47,889,233, the last purchases of the run. Another 19 transactions in that window reverted, so their ETH never arrived and they are not counted. Every indexed transaction from then to the terminal block, 849 in all, carried no ETH. From block 47,891,791, where Level 39 opens, to the terminal block no 100-block window shows a rise in the game's ETH balance, which is 8,619.7 from day 50 to the end. The contract's total balance is its ETH plus its stETH, checked at days 45, 50, 137. After day 50 the only part that grows is the stETH, from 8,224.8 to 8,338.8.

Numbering and timing. Every chart numbers a level by the tickets being bought, so Level 42's stall is the purchase phase at contract level 41. A level's transition lands at the day boundary, so the pools shown at target are the last snapshot before it: day 47 for Level 38, whose transition came on day 48. The five stall figures share one ETH-to-pixel scale.
